About

Strategy to execution, measured end to end

I've spent four years building pipeline for B2B SaaS companies in Bengaluru and lead flow for service businesses in Saudi Arabia.

My work starts with the buyer: who they are, where they spend time and what makes them ask for a demo. Then I pick the channels, launch the campaigns and follow every lead through to sales.

I've defined ICPs and personas, owned lead qualification and handoff, led small teams, and built GA4, UTM and Looker Studio reporting so everyone can see which source produced which lead. I use ChatGPT and Claude every day, and I keep up with AI search (AEO and GEO) alongside classic SEO.

How I work

Four steps on every engagement

01

Research

Define the ICP and personas, audit the website, tracking and current channels, and find where leads leak.

02

Plan

Build the demand plan: channel mix, budget, offers, campaign calendar and the KPIs we'll report on.

03

Launch

Run the campaigns across ads, email, LinkedIn, SEO and webinars, with clean UTMs and a clear handoff to sales.

04

Optimise

Weekly A/B tests and Looker Studio reports. Move budget to what produces qualified leads, not just clicks.
